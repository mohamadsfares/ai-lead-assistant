# Services

## Roof Replacement
Full tear-off and replacement of residential roofs. We install asphalt shingle roofs and standing-seam metal roofs. Every replacement includes removal of old materials, inspection and repair of the roof deck, new underlayment, new flashing, ridge vents, and full cleanup.

## Roof Repair
Repairs for leaks, missing or damaged shingles, flashing problems, and damaged vents. Most repairs are completed in one day.

## Storm Damage and Insurance Claims
We inspect roofs after hail and wind storms and document damage with photos. We can meet your insurance adjuster on site and provide a detailed estimate for your claim. The homeowner is responsible for filing the claim with their insurance company.

## Gutters
Installation of seamless aluminum gutters and downspouts, gutter guards, and gutter repair.

## Siding
Installation and replacement of vinyl siding and fiber cement siding, including trim and soffits.

## Windows
Replacement of residential windows with energy-efficient double-pane vinyl windows.

## Kitchen Remodeling
Full and partial kitchen remodels: cabinets, countertops, backsplash, flooring, lighting, and layout changes. We work with licensed electricians and plumbers for the electrical and plumbing parts of a remodel.

## Bathroom Remodeling
Full and partial bathroom remodels: tub-to-shower conversions, walk-in showers, vanities, tile, flooring, and fixtures.

## Services We Do Not Offer
- Solar panel installation
- Swimming pools and decks
- HVAC (heating and air conditioning) installation or repair
- Plumbing or electrical work that is not part of a remodel
- Commercial or industrial buildings
- Flat roofs on commercial buildings
