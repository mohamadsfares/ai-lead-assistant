# Service Areas

## Core Service Area (no travel fee)
- Columbus
- Dublin
- Westerville
- Hilliard
- Grove City
- Gahanna
- Worthington
- Upper Arlington
- Reynoldsburg
- Pickerington

## Extended Service Area (travel fee applies)
We also serve the following towns with a one-time travel fee of $75 per project:
- Delaware
- Newark
- Marysville
- Lancaster

Emergency tarping within 24 hours is only guaranteed in the core service area. In the extended service area, emergency response is within 48 hours.

## Outside Our Service Area
We do not serve homes outside the towns listed above. This includes Cincinnati, Cleveland, Dayton, and Toledo. Visitors outside our area can ask us for a referral, but we cannot provide estimates or work there.
