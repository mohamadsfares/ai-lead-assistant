# Price Ranges

All prices are typical ranges for 2026 in our service area. They are estimates, not quotes. The final price is confirmed in a written estimate after a free on-site inspection, because it depends on the size of the home, the materials chosen, and the condition of the existing structure.

## Inspection and Estimates
- On-site inspection: free
- Written estimate: free, delivered within 48 hours of the inspection

## Roofing
- Asphalt shingle roof replacement (typical home of 1,500 to 2,500 sq ft): $9,500 to $22,000
- Standing-seam metal roof replacement (same size range): $18,000 to $40,000
- Roof repair: $350 to $1,800 for most repairs
- Emergency tarping: $250 to $600

## Gutters
- Seamless gutters and downspouts for a whole home: $1,200 to $3,500
- Gutter guards: $900 to $2,400

## Siding
- Vinyl siding for a whole home: $9,000 to $20,000
- Fiber cement siding for a whole home: $14,000 to $32,000

## Windows
- Window replacement: $650 to $1,300 per window, installed

## Remodeling
- Kitchen remodel: $18,000 to $60,000
- Bathroom remodel: $9,000 to $30,000
- Tub-to-shower conversion only: $6,500 to $12,000

## Payment Terms
- A deposit of 10% is due when the contract is signed.
- The remaining balance is due when the project is completed and the customer has approved the final walkthrough.
- We accept checks, bank transfers, and all major credit cards. Credit card payments over $5,000 have a 2.5% processing fee.
