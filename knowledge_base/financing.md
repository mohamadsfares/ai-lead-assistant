# Financing

## Financing Partner
We offer financing through our partner, Buckeye Home Lending (a fictional lender for this demo). Financing is subject to credit approval by the lender, not by Cedar Ridge.

## Financing Options
- 0% interest for 12 months for approved customers, if the full balance is paid within 12 months.
- Fixed-rate plans from 5 to 10 years, with rates set by the lender based on credit.
- Minimum project size for financing: $3,000.

## How to Apply
Customers can apply during or after their free inspection. The application takes about 10 minutes and a decision is usually given the same day.

## Insurance Claim Projects
For storm damage projects paid by insurance, the homeowner pays their insurance deductible. Financing can be used to cover the deductible or any upgrades not covered by insurance.
