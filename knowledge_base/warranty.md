# Warranty

## Roofing Warranty
- Workmanship warranty on roof replacements: 10 years. This covers problems caused by our installation, such as leaks from improper flashing.
- Manufacturer warranty on asphalt shingles: 25 to 50 years, depending on the shingle line chosen.
- Manufacturer warranty on metal roofing panels: 40 years.
- Roof repairs: 2-year workmanship warranty on the repaired area.

## Siding, Gutters and Windows Warranty
- Workmanship warranty: 5 years.
- Manufacturer warranties on materials apply as provided by each manufacturer.

## Remodeling Warranty
- Workmanship warranty on kitchen and bathroom remodels: 2 years.
- Appliances and fixtures are covered by their own manufacturer warranties.

## What Is Not Covered
- Damage from storms, falling trees, or other events after installation (this is usually covered by homeowner's insurance).
- Damage caused by work done by other contractors on the same area.
- Normal wear and tear.
