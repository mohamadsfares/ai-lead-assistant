# Company Overview

## About Cedar Ridge
Cedar Ridge Roofing & Renovation is a family-owned home-improvement company based in Columbus, Ohio. We have served central Ohio homeowners since 2009. We focus on residential homes only; we do not take commercial or industrial projects.

## Licenses and Insurance
- Licensed contractor in the State of Ohio and registered with the City of Columbus.
- Fully insured: general liability insurance and workers' compensation for every crew member.
- Certificates of insurance are available to any customer on request.
