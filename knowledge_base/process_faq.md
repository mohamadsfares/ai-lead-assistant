# Our Process and Frequently Asked Questions

## Our Process
1. **Request:** the customer contacts us by phone, email, or the website chat.
2. **Free inspection:** a project advisor visits the home, usually within 3 to 5 business days.
3. **Written estimate:** delivered within 48 hours of the inspection.
4. **Contract and deposit:** the customer signs the contract and pays a 10% deposit.
5. **Scheduling:** we order materials and schedule the start date.
6. **Work:** our crew completes the project.
7. **Final walkthrough:** the customer inspects the work with the project advisor before the final payment.

## Typical Project Timelines
- Roof replacement: 1 to 3 days of work on site.
- Roof repair: usually 1 day.
- Gutters: 1 day.
- Siding: 1 to 2 weeks.
- Window replacement: 1 to 2 days for a typical home.
- Bathroom remodel: 2 to 4 weeks.
- Kitchen remodel: 4 to 8 weeks.

## FAQ

### Do you handle building permits?
Yes. We apply for all required building permits, and the permit cost is included in the estimate.

### Do I need to be home during the work?
For roofing, gutters, and siding, you do not need to be home, but someone must be available by phone. For remodels and window replacement, someone must give our crew access to the inside of the home.

### Do you work in winter?
Yes. We install roofs year-round when the temperature is above 40°F (about 4°C) and it is not raining or snowing. Remodeling work continues all year.

### Do you clean up after the job?
Yes. Every project includes daily cleanup. For roofing, we use magnetic sweepers to pick up nails from the yard and driveway.

### What about my pets?
For safety, we ask that pets stay indoors or away from the work area during roofing and siding work.

### Can I choose the materials and colors?
Yes. Your project advisor brings samples to the inspection, and you choose shingle, siding, and cabinet colors before the contract is signed.

### Do you offer free estimates?
Yes. The inspection and the written estimate are always free, with no obligation.

### How long does the free inspection take?
A roofing inspection takes less than an hour: we check the roof, take photos, and show you material samples. A remodel inspection takes about 1 to 2 hours because we discuss the design with you, and we send a 3D design together with your written estimate within 48 hours.
